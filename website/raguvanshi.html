About Us

Raghuvanshi Group is a diversified business group with decades of experience across manufacturing, trading, and industrial supply sectors. Built on the values of quality, trust, and long-term relationships, the group has steadily grown into a reliable name serving industrial and commercial clients.

With a strong foundation that began in timber and wood processing, Raghuvanshi Group has expanded its operations to include thermocol , fabric manufacturing, and industrial trading. Each business under the group operates with a commitment to precision, consistency, and customer satisfaction.

The group focuses on delivering practical, cost-effective, and durable solutions that meet the evolving needs of industries such as packaging, construction, manufacturing, and logistics. Backed by skilled manpower, modern processes, and ethical business practices, Raghuvanshi Group continues to build lasting partnerships and contribute to sustainable industrial growth.

Our Businesses

Raghuvansi Saw Mill

Established in 1980, Raghuvansi Saw Mill specializes in high-quality timber processing and wooden products. We manufacture wooden pallets, packing boxes, crates, cable drums, and provide customized wood job work for industrial and commercial needs.

Bhavana Traders

Founded in 2001, Bhavana Traders is a trusted supplier of engineering, hardware, and machinery tools. We deal in saw mill tools, pipe fitting tools, and general machinery equipment, serving workshops, factories, and industrial clients with reliable solutions

Bansary Thermocol

Bansary Thermocol specializes in manufacturing premium thermocol (EPS) products. Our offerings include thermocol boxes, sheets, molds, and customized packaging solutions, widely used for protection, insulation, and industrial packing applications.

Bansari Polymers

Bansari Polymers focuses on polymer and fabric-based manufacturing solutions. We deliver durable, high-quality polymer and textile products designed for industrial, packaging, and manufacturing applications with consistent performance and reliability.
