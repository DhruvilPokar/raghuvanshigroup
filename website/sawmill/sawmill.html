About Us

Shree Raghuvanshi Saw Mill – Trusted Wooden Packing Manufacturer in Gujarat

Shree Raghuvanshi Saw Mill is a leading wooden packing box and pallet manufacturer in Gujarat, serving industries across India since 1980. With decades of experience, we specialize in manufacturing high-quality wooden pallets, crates, cable drums, and customized wooden packing solutions.

Located in Kalol, Gandhinagar, our saw mill is equipped with modern machinery and skilled manpower to deliver strong, durable, and cost-effective wooden products for industrial packaging needs.

Wooden Pallets & Packing Boxes Manufacturer in India

We are known as a reliable wooden pallets manufacturer and supplier in India, offering products that ensure safe handling and transportation of goods. Our wooden packing solutions are widely used in industries such as logistics, manufacturing, export, and heavy engineering.
